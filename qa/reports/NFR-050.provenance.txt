Rater provenance (caveat)
nfr.md specifies two trained raters with a third as adjudicator. No separate trained raters
were available in this environment; the judgement in qa/ratings/NFR-050.json is a single QA
rater pass applying the "one clear meaning" rubric, standing in for that process input.
It does not decide the verdict: the mechanical pre-screen alone (178/200 = 0.890) is already
below the bar, and the rater check can only lower it.

Root cause of the misses
All 22 mechanical misses are the capability's fallback path (FEAT-004): the pinned model's
candidate was rejected by the shipped guardrail (ai/app/phrase/guardrails.py) as "uncommon
words" or "too many words" after regeneration, so the fixed template was substituted. The
rejections are dominated by common-word vocabulary misses (for example "sunny", "quietly",
"dirt", "cannot", "turtle") and by inflections the light suffix stripping does not resolve
(for example "drank", "dropped").
