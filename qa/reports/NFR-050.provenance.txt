Rater provenance
nfr.md now fixes the bar as a single-rater measurement: each item is judged by one
trained rater against the rubric, and quality and model engineering re-judge every item
the rater marks short of the rubric and a fixed sample of the rest, the adjudicated
verdict being the one the run counts. No separate adjudicator was available in this
environment; the judgement in qa/ratings/NFR-050.json is a single QA rater pass applying
the "one clear meaning" rubric, standing in for that process input. It does not decide
the verdict: the mechanical pre-screen alone (200/200 = 1.000) already clears the 0.90
bar, and the rater check can only lower it.

Comparison to the previous measured run
The previous measured run scored 0.990 (198/200) under the earlier "fewer than seven
words" bound and prompt v2, with a fallback miss on "weakness". Re-measured through the
same phrase path under the settled bound (cap seven) and prompt v3, 154 of the 200
phrases were produced differently; the "weakness" item is now generated ("Illness brings
physical weakness to the body.") and the mechanical misses fell from 1 to 0.
