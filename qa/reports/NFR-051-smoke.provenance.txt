NFR-051 word-clip live smoke — provenance

Bar       docs/Scenova/nfr.md, NFR-051 (FEAT-005). Metric: share of clips a rater
          judges to depict the phrase's meaning. Threshold >= 0.80. This smoke does
          not measure that bar: it is the live-path smoke that must pass before the
          judged NFR-051 run is worth spending (nfr.md "Model Quality & Cost";
          DELIVERY.md D-020 leaves NFR-051 unmeasured and the generation path's
          release flagged until it is).
Model     gemini-omni-1.1-flash — the pinned video model for word clips (nfr.md
          "Model Quality & Cost"; architecture.md "Model Version & Fallback").
Run date  2026-10-13.

What ran
One clip through the shipped C-005 surface: the real `app.main:app` router, the
real `app.clip.service.ClipService` (duration clamp, prompt artifact
word-clip-v2), and the real `app.provider.GeminiClipModel` against the pinned
model. Only the object-storage boundary — the documented `ClipStore` protocol —
is substituted by an in-memory recording store, because the gate environment
holds no Cloud Storage credential; the recorded bytes are what the smoke
measures. The clip's input is the first item of the NFR-051 golden set
(qa/golden/word_clip.jsonl): "a key in the door", target duration 4s.

Artifacts
- qa/clip_smoke.py               The smoke harness and the `python -m qa.clip_smoke`
                                 entry point. It parses the returned MP4 for its
                                 real container duration, resolution, and tracks.
- qa/test_clip_smoke.py          The model-marked gate checks over the harness.
- qa/reports/NFR-051-smoke.json  This run: the served contract, the measured media,
                                 and whether the clip met the contract.

Result
- status 201; body carries every C-005 field.
- served durationSeconds 4.0; resolution 360p; hasAudio true; synthIdWatermark true.
- media: 640x360, video and audio tracks, container duration 4.032s, 1,325,688 bytes.
- Exactly one clip was produced and stored. The clip met the contract.

Note on the duration
The contract field is the value the service serves; the encoded clip's real
container duration is measured separately because a provider that ignored the
requested length would still leave the served field plausible. The measured
4.032s trails the 4s target by under one frame of the container's frame grid, so
the smoke allows one frame of encoder rounding around the three-to-four-second
range and records the exact value here. The pipeline withholds any clip whose
served duration falls outside three to four seconds (services/pipeline/app), so
the product's own gate is the served field; this smoke adds the media check.

Not covered here

- The provider-declines fallback and the ten-second background hand-off are
