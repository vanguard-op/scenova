Golden sets for the model quality bar (docs/Scenova/nfr.md, NFR-050 to NFR-053).

These are the fixed evaluation inputs the judged bar requires. They are curated
by hand, not sampled from model output, and they are not tuned to the pinned
model: they are a representative spread of the inputs each capability serves.

gloss_phrase.jsonl  (NFR-050)  200 items  {"lemma", "partOfSpeech", "senseHint"}
  Inputs to gloss-phrase generation (FEAT-004). Composition: 90 nouns (50
  concrete, 40 abstract), 50 verbs, 40 adjectives, 20 adverbs. Each item is one
  distinct sense, with a short hint in the shape the capability contract takes.

word_clip.jsonl     (NFR-051)  100 items  {"word", "phrase"}
  Inputs to word-clip generation (FEAT-005), spanning concrete and abstract:
  60 concrete words and 40 abstract words, each paired with the gloss phrase the
  clip is asked to depict.

phrase_clip.jsonl   (NFR-053)   50 items  {"phrase"}
  Inputs to phrase-clip generation (FEAT-007): 25 idioms and 25 phrasal verbs.

Item identity: an item is addressed by the join of its input fields (see
ai/quality/bar.py, item_key). Ratings, recorded runs, and reported failures all
use that identity, so a rater's verdict attaches to the exact item it judged.

Judging: the bar is judged by two trained raters with a third as adjudicator
(nfr.md). A rating is a boolean, or {"raters": [bool, ...], "adjudicator": bool}
when the raters disagree. NFR-050's mechanical rubric (fewer than seven words,
common words) is checked in the run before the rater's meaning check.

A run of the evaluation is `python -m quality --bar NFR-050` from the repo root
with ai/ on the path; see ai/quality/__main__.py. A run against the pinned model
needs the provider credential or a recorded replay.
